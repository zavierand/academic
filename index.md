What’s good y’all! My name is Zavier Andrianarivo, and I’m a recent NYU graduate with a B.A. in Computer Science and a minor in Mathematics. This site is a home for my academic work, research interests, creative projects, and anything in between!

My previous work focused on scaling zero-shot learning frameworks for generalizable robot behaviors. My current research explores latent world models for control and planning, with downstream applications in long-horizon robot behavior, autonomous driving, and broader embodied intelligence agents.

Currently, I'm a research intern at [NYU CILVR](https://wp.nyu.edu/cilvr/), where I have been working under the supervision of [Jeff Cui](https://jeffcui.com/) and [Lerrel Pinto](https://www.lerrelpinto.com/).

Feel free to explore my website -- containing notes from different classes I've taken so far, hobbies I enjoy, to projects I've built, papers that I've read, and stuff I look to explore! Feel free to connect with me through any of my social handles in the footer - though I'll probably respond fastest to an email.
